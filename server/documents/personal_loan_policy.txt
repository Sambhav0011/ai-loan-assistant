Personal Loan Policy
This sample document contains fictional policies for demonstrating document chunking.

1. Eligibility
Applicants must be 21–60 years old and have a minimum monthly income of ₹25,000.

2. Loan Amount
The minimum loan amount is ₹50,000, and the maximum loan amount is ₹10,00,000.

3. Interest Rate
The annual interest rate is 10%–16%, depending on the applicant's credit profile.

4. Required Documents
Applicants must provide identity proof, address proof, a PAN card, and salary slips for the last three months.

5. Repayment
The repayment period is 12–60 months.

6. Processing Fee
The processing fee is up to 2% of the loan amount.
